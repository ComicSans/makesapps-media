## Description

Master photography in one year with a single, focused ritual: one unique creative prompt every day. Build your eye, boost your confidence, and see how thousands around the world interpret the same spark.

• Improve fast: Daily prompts force consistent practice so your composition, timing, and vision get better every week.
• Global inspiration: See how thousands of photographers interpret the same theme and expand your creative perspective.
• Create privately: Your photos stay under your control—no tracking, no data mining, and no pressure to post.
• Accessible to all levels: Prompts work for phone shooters and DSLR pros—grow at your own pace.

How it works
Each day you receive one unique photography prompt. Shoot your interpretation, keep it on your device, and optionally share with the global gallery when you choose. No repeats, no algorithms - just one new challenge every day to keep you improving.

Who it's for
Photographers and visual creators who want structured, daily practice without the noise of social feeds. Ideal for hobbyists, students, and pros sharpening their eye.

Privacy by Design: Your Data, Your Art
We believe your creativity shouldn't be a product for data brokers. 365 Lens offers you total freedom:
• Local Storage: Everything is saved on your device, not our servers.
• Full Control: You decide what to share, when, and where.
• Pure Experience: No tracking, no data monetization, and no AI-generated content.
• No paywall, no ads: Every single feature is free, forever.
