## Description

A short moment between reaching for your phone and opening the app: PauseFirst gives you a small task before selected apps open. If you really need the app, you solve it in seconds. If you're just reaching out of habit, you notice, and often let it go.

HOW IT WORKS
• Choose the apps or categories that should get a pause.
• Open one of them and a block screen appears with "Start task".
• PauseFirst opens with a short task. Solved? The app is free for 15 minutes, then it locks again.
• Each further unlock on the same day takes a little longer. At midnight the day starts over with the first unlock.
• Prefer to go direct? In PauseFirst, tap "Start task" next to the app. The Control Center control takes you to that list in one tap.

12 TASKS TO BREAK THE AUTOPILOT
Math chain, Stroop, Digits backwards, Anagram, Letter Hunter, Typing, Tiles, Precision Tap, Slow swipe, Breath hold, Gyro Balance and Table lock. Some need your head, some a steady hand, some a long breath. In "Practice tasks" you can try every task at any level without anything counting. Tasks are available in English and German.

SCHEDULE AND STATISTICS
• Block on a schedule, for example only during work or in the evening.
• Statistics for today and the last 7 days: unlocks per day, by weekday and time of day.

FOR EMERGENCIES
One emergency unlock a day frees an app for 5 minutes without a task. It doesn't count as an unlock, but it shows up in your statistics.

SOLVABLE FOR EVERYONE
Anything you physically can't solve, for example because of motor skills or color vision, you exclude for free under Accessibility.

EVERYTHING STAYS ON YOUR IPHONE
No account, no server, no ads, no tracking. Only iOS sees which apps you block. PauseFirst collects no data.

FRICTIONBLOCK PRO

Pro is a one-time purchase, no subscription, with Family Sharing. PauseFirst is made by one person; Pro supports its further development.

TO BE HONEST
PauseFirst isn't a safe, just a short pause. Removing PauseFirst's access in iOS Settings under Screen Time lifts all blocks. That's on purpose: you stay in control.

REQUIREMENTS
iPhone with iOS 27 and Screen Time. PauseFirst is meant for self-control on your own iPhone. On devices in Family Sharing, a parent manages Screen Time.
