## Promotional text

Six new kits, Markdown journal export, and an optional instant roll. Turn your best prompts into playable gamebooks with our open-source Story Weaver.

## Description

Plot Spark ignites your next idea - with a single roll.

Whether you're working on a novel, a short film, a tabletop RPG campaign, or just need a story for tonight: Plot Spark instantly delivers a character, a conflict, and a setting - random, unexpected, and inspiring.

HOW IT WORKS
Pick a kit, roll the dice - and get a complete story prompt with character, conflict, setting, and a wild twist. Sounds too simple? That's exactly the point. Prefer instant results over a dice animation? Turn the roll animation off and get straight to the prompt.

ELEVEN KITS, EVERY GENRE
Plot Spark ships with eleven curated kits - from neon-soaked noir and cosmic comedy to high-seas adventure, Cold War espionage, and Victorian hollow-earth expeditions. And you can create your own kits and fill every slot with your own ideas - tuned to exactly the tone you need.

JOURNAL & MARKDOWN EXPORT
Save every hit straight to your journal. Write notes, develop the idea, or let it simmer - your best prompts won't get lost. Export any entry, or your whole journal, as clean Markdown and keep working in your favorite writing app.

FROM SPARK TO GAMEBOOK
Your exported prompts are plain Markdown - the same format Story Weaver speaks, our free open-source language for writing branching gamebooks with choices, dice, and fights. Grow a rolled prompt into a full playable adventure: github.com/ComicSans/story-weaver

NO SUBSCRIPTION. NO ACCOUNT.
Plot Spark needs no internet connection, no login, and no subscription. Your ideas stay on your device.
