## description

Your mood has a color. Color Mood Circle lets you capture how you feel in one playful gesture: drag the pin around the color wheel until it feels right, then let go.

CAPTURE A MOOD IN SECONDS
• Twelve moods on one wheel, from Peaceful, Calm and Happy to Angry, Sad, Exhausted and Tired
• Your mood shows in large type above the wheel, while the center blends the colors as you move
• Gentle haptics as you pass from one mood to the next
• Tap the emoji to show or hide the glowing color blobs

YOUR JOURNEY SO FAR
Every mood you log today lines up on the home screen, so you can see how your day has moved.

SEE YOUR PATTERNS
• Look back over today, the past week, month or year, or everything at once
• A mood distribution shows which feelings came up most often
• A short quote to go with it; pull down for a new one
• Remove any entry you don't want to keep

NEW: THE JOURNAL
• A monthly calendar where each day carries the color of its last mood
• Move between months or jump straight back to today
• Open any day to see every mood you logged and when
• Write a note for each day; it saves as you type

MADE FOR iPHONE
• Rebuilt from the ground up for iOS 26
• Designed for Dark and Light mode
• Works with VoiceOver and Dynamic Type, and respects Reduce Motion

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no sign-up. Your moods and notes stay on your iPhone.

## release_notes

Color Mood Circle 2.0 is rebuilt from the ground up for iOS 26.

• New Journal tab: a monthly calendar that shows each day in the color of its last mood
• A page for every day with all of its moods and a note of your own, saved as you type
• Two new moods on the wheel: Exhausted and Tired
• A fresh design with fluid motion and gentle haptics, in Dark and Light mode
• Support for VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion
• Your moods from earlier versions come along automatically
