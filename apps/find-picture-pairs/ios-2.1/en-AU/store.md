## Description

Picture matching game, reimagined: Classic pair-finding with surprising twists!

I love games that are simple yet still surprise.

Everyone knows the classic picture matching game from childhood - but for this app I've come up with some special tricks that challenge kids and adults alike.

Fair, honest & secure
Pay once, play forever: Guaranteed no ads, no subscription, no tracking, and no hidden in-app purchases.

100% offline:
Runs completely without internet on iPhone and iPad. Your data stays exactly where it belongs - on your device.

For a quick round or long game nights
8 lovingly designed theme sets: From nature, beach and cars to symbols, comic and real photos.
A range of board sizes: From an easy 3×4 for a quick start to a large 6×8 with 48 cards.
Your design: Customize the game with 18 accent colors to suit your taste.

Four varied game modes
Classic: Relaxed, at your own pace with no time pressure.
Beat the Clock: The timer starts on your first tap - can you earn the gold medal?
Rotation: After each turn, two hidden neighboring cards secretly swap places.
Triplets: Find three matching cards instead of just two.

Comfort & extras
Achievements to discover and collect.
Smart hints when you're completely stuck.
Customizable with mute and extra-fast speed mode.

Whether for a quick break or winding down on the couch: Mix theme, mode and size again and again.

Happy puzzling!
Tobias
