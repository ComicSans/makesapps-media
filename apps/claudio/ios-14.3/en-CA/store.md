## Promotional text

Audiobooks, podcasts and music from your NAS, Jellyfin, Navidrome, Audiobookshelf or iCloud Drive. Free to start. No account, no ads, no tracking.

## Description

Your audiobooks live on your NAS, as M4B or MP3. CLAUDIO plays them with chapters, stops at the end of a chapter with the sleep timer, and picks up right there the next evening.

I build CLAUDIO on my own, so let me be up front about what is in which tier: CLAUDIO Ultra, a subscription that includes Pro, connects your NAS and other servers. Chapters, the sleep timer and the other listening features are also available as a one-time purchase, CLAUDIO Pro. To get both without a subscription, buy CLAUDIO Pro+ once.

START FREE
• Play music and audiobooks from local files and iCloud Drive.
• Subscribe to and stream podcasts by URL or Apple Podcasts search.
• Organize with Favorites and Smart Lists such as Recently Added, and use Shuffle and Smart Shuffle.
• All themes, including the high-contrast one.

LISTEN BETTER - CLAUDIO PRO, ONE-TIME
Buy once, no subscription.
• Audiobooks: chapters from M4B and tagged MP3, a sleep timer to the end of the chapter or book, playback speed with pitch correction, silence trimming. After a pause, CLAUDIO resumes a few seconds earlier so you don't land mid-sentence. If the sleep timer stopped too soon, a knock resumes playback (Knock to Resume).
• Podcasts: download episodes, skip intros and outros, OPML import and export, plus chapters and the sleep timer as with audiobooks.
• Music: Album Wall and LP Mode, 10-band EQ, ReplayGain, Gapless, Crossfade, Stereo Widening, volume normalization, animated visualizers and synced lyrics. Scrobbling to ListenBrainz and Last.fm.
• Read along: downloaded audiobooks and podcasts are transcribed on device in supported languages, and the text follows the audio.
• Widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen, controls for Control Centre.

MATCH YOUR HEADPHONES
Pick your model from more than 6,000 headphones, and CLAUDIO builds the matching EQ curve from the open AutoEq measurement data. Listening to it is free; saving it takes CLAUDIO Pro.

CLAUDIO ULTRA - MONTHLY OR YEARLY
Ultra connects every self-hosted source: NAS/SMB, Jellyfin, Audiobookshelf, Subsonic and Navidrome, DLNA, WebDAV and SFTP. While active, Ultra includes everything in Pro.
• Offline cache and pinning: download titles from your server and listen without a connection.
• CarPlay for downloaded and local titles.
• Send local and downloaded files to Google Cast, DLNA/UPnP and Sonos.
• Favorites that sync with Navidrome, Jellyfin and Audiobookshelf.
• Statistics and background library refresh.
The first scan of a large library takes a while. It keeps running when you leave the app.

OWN IT FOREVER - CLAUDIO PRO+, ONE-TIME
Don't want a subscription at all? Pro+ unlocks Pro and Ultra permanently with a single purchase, every remote source included, with no recurring payment. Pro+ can be shared through Family Sharing. Existing owners of the former CLAUDIO Pro one-time purchase already have Pro+ automatically.

PRIVACY
No CLAUDIO account. No ads. No analytics SDKs. No telemetry. Your library, playback history and podcast subscriptions stay on your device. The app connects only to the sources you choose, such as NAS/SMB, Jellyfin, iCloud Drive, RSS feeds or Apple Podcasts search. The App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected".

MADE BY ONE PERSON

## What's new

New in CLAUDIO 14.3

• iCloud Sync: settings, sources, podcast subscriptions, lists, favourites and listening positions stay in sync across your devices. After a reinstall, the guide offers to restore from iCloud. With CLAUDIO Pro.
