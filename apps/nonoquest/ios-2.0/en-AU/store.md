## Promotional text

Solve 130 handcrafted picture logic puzzles across thirteen story campaigns with a retro handheld feel. Every one works out with logic alone.

## Description

NonoQuest blends classic picture logic puzzles with a retro handheld presentation and light adventure storytelling.

Every puzzle can be solved with logic alone — you never have to guess. Mistakes surface early, and any move can be undone.

Travel through thirteen themed campaigns, uncover pixel-art scenes, and solve 130 handcrafted nonogram boards ranging from compact warmups to larger expert challenges.

What you get:
- 130 handcrafted nonogram puzzles across 13 campaigns
- A daily puzzle, the same one for everybody worldwide
- An endless mode in five grid sizes
- An album that keeps every solved picture in view
- A guided tutorial for new players
- Four-step help that explains the technique instead of giving the answer away
- Seven retro display modes inspired by classic hardware
- Offline play with saved progress, no ads and no in-app purchases

The game is fully playable with VoiceOver, Switch Control and a keyboard. Every grid cell speaks its position, its state and its clue numbers.

Whether you call them nonograms, picross, or picture logic puzzles, NonoQuest focuses on readable controls, satisfying reveals, and a distinct retro atmosphere.

## What's new

Three new campaigns with 30 additional puzzles, plus a daily puzzle, an endless mode, and an album that collects every picture you solve. A toggleable sound has been added as well — off by default.
