## Description

NonoQuest blends classic picture logic puzzles with a retro handheld presentation and light adventure storytelling.

Every puzzle can be solved with logic alone — you never have to guess. Mistakes surface early, and any move can be undone.

Travel through 19 themed campaigns and solve 190 handcrafted puzzles from 5×5 to 20×20, including colour puzzles where the logic plays out in several colours. The ten pictures of each campaign come together as one large chapter picture.

What you get:
- 190 handcrafted nonogram puzzles across 19 campaigns
- Colour puzzles in which every clue number has its own colour
- A large mosaic picture for every chapter
- Up to three stars per puzzle: one for solving it, one for using no help, one for making no mistakes
- Hints that explain the next logical step, plus a catalogue of techniques with examples
- A daily puzzle, the same one for everybody worldwide, with a streak and monthly badges
- An endless mode in five grid sizes
- An album that keeps every solved picture in view
- A guided tutorial for new players
- Seven retro display modes inspired by classic hardware
- Chiptune music for every campaign; sound is off by default
- Offline play with saved progress, no ads and no tracking

You can play First Steps, the colour campaign Paint Box, the tutorial, the daily puzzle and the techniques without buying anything. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks all other campaigns and endless mode. There is no subscription. If you bought NonoQuest before version 3.0, you get everything without buying again.

The game is fully playable with VoiceOver, Switch Control and a keyboard. Every grid cell speaks its position, its state and its clue numbers.

Whether you call them nonograms, Japanese crosswords, or picture logic puzzles, NonoQuest focuses on readable controls, satisfying reveals, and a distinct retro atmosphere.
